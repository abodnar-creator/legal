Disposable – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how the Disposable app for iPhone ("Disposable" or "the app") handles your information. Disposable is made by Adam Bodnar, an independent developer based in Slovakia ("we", "us").

The short version

  • Disposable has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app doesn't send your photos or any other personal data to us. We don't run any servers that receive data from the app.
  • Everything the app creates stays on your iPhone, unless you choose to save a print to Photos or send it to someone.
  • Disposable Pro purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details.

Who is responsible

Adam Bodnar, Slovakia. Email: contact@bdproductions.eu

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Adam Bodnar is the controller of any personal data we process. In practice, that's only messages you send us (see "If you contact us").

What the app keeps on your iPhone

The app stores the following only on your device, in storage that belongs to the app and to its widgets. We can't access it.

  • Your photos. Each shot is kept as a developed print and as a "negative": a smaller copy of the original photo that's used to make reprints. If you reprint a photo, the first print is kept so you can switch back to it.
  • Roll details, such as the film type, when each photo was taken, whether the flash fired, and when a roll went to the lab and will be ready.
  • Rolls friends send you: their photos, the sender's name, the names of the people who took each photo, and an optional short note.
  • The name you type when you send a roll, so you don't have to type it again, and whether the date stamp is on.
  • Your Disposable Pro status: whether you have Pro and, for a subscription, when the current period ends.
  • Small copies of prints and roll progress used by the widgets, the Live Activity and notifications.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera, to take photos. The camera only runs while the camera screen is open. The app takes still photos only. It doesn't record video or sound, and it doesn't ask for access to the microphone.
  • Photos (add only), to save prints to your photo library when you choose to. With this permission the app can add photos, but it can't see your library.
  • Notifications, to tell you when a roll has developed. These notifications are created on your iPhone, not sent from a server. A notification can show the name of the friend who sent the roll and a small picture of one print, including on your Lock Screen. You can change this in Settings > Notifications > Disposable.

If you add Disposable's widgets or allow its Live Activity, they show prints and roll progress on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

The app doesn't ask for your location, your contacts, your microphone, or permission to track you. You can change permissions at any time in Settings > Disposable. Without camera access you can't take new photos, but you can still view and share the rolls you have.

Details inside photo files

The app never records your location. Prints, double exposures and negatives are saved without hidden camera details such as the camera model, lens, capture time or location. If the date stamp is on, the date is printed visibly on the photo itself. When you save a print to Photos, the app sets the photo's date to the time it was taken.

Sending rolls and sharing prints

When you send a roll or share a print, you choose where it goes in Apple's share sheet, for example AirDrop, Messages, Mail or another app. The app creates the file on your iPhone and hands it to the service you chose. We don't receive a copy.

A roll file contains:

  • the prints on the roll (never the negatives);
  • the name you typed as the sender, and the names of the people who took each photo;
  • your optional note;
  • the film type, when each photo was taken, whether the flash fired, whether a photo is a double exposure, and when the roll was sent;
  • a random code that lets the app recognise the same roll if it's opened twice.

Your sender name also appears in the file's name, in the share subject and, where the service supports it, in a short message that goes with the file.

Roll files aren't encrypted. A "sealed" roll is only hidden inside Disposable until it develops. Anyone who gets the file could open the photos with other tools, so only send rolls to people you trust.

The service you choose (for example Apple, for AirDrop and Messages) handles the transfer under its own terms and privacy policy. People you send rolls or prints to can keep them and pass them on.

When someone sends you a roll, the app only adds it if you accept it. Either way, it then deletes the incoming copy of the file that iOS gave it.

Disposable Pro purchases

Disposable Pro is sold through Apple's App Store as a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment. We don't receive your name, your email address, your Apple Account details or your payment information.

The app checks your purchases on your iPhone with Apple's StoreKit, and it keeps only whether Pro is active and, for a subscription, when the current period ends.

  • To manage or cancel a subscription, go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, or see Apple's guide: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223
  • Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Apple gives us sales and subscription reports. They don't include your name or contact details.

What Apple may share with us

If you've turned on Share With App Developers (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and statistics about how the app is used with us, in a form that doesn't identify you. We use them only to fix problems and improve the app.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and whatever you write. We use this only to answer you and deal with your request.

  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering messages about the app (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) and, where you ask us to do something, taking the steps you requested (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Our email provider, Hostinger, stores messages on our behalf.
  • Retention: we keep emails for up to 12 months after the conversation ends, unless we need them longer to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and the app contains no third-party code (SDKs) that collects data.
  • We don't create accounts or profiles.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Everything the app stores stays on your iPhone until you delete it.
  • To delete a roll, touch and hold it in the Darkroom and choose Throw away roll. This deletes its prints, its negatives, and any first prints kept after reprints. For a roll that's still in the camera, choose Finish roll early first.
  • To change or clear the sender name the app remembers, edit it the next time you send a roll.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your iPhone, including the widgets' data.
  • Some copies live outside the app and aren't deleted this way: prints you saved to Photos, files you sent to other people, and backups of your iPhone (iCloud Backup or a computer), which follow your backup settings.
  • Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Apple Account settings, as described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access, correct or delete your personal data;
  • restrict or object to how it's used;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time.

The app keeps your data only on your iPhone, so you control it there directly. For anything we hold, such as emails you've sent us, write to contact@bdproductions.eu. We'll reply within one month.

If you're in the EU, you also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Slovakia, that's the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky), https://dataprotection.gov.sk/en/. You can also contact the authority in the country where you live or work.

Children

Disposable isn't directed at children under 13. The app doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

California and other US states

We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising, as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar state laws.

Security

What the app stores is kept in its own protected storage on your iPhone and is covered by the built-in protections of iOS, including the encryption iOS applies to your data when your iPhone has a passcode.
