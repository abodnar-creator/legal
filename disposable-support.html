Disposable – Support

Need help with Disposable? Email contact@bdproductions.eu and we'll get back to you. If something isn't working, tell us your iPhone model and iOS version.

Disposable is made by Adam Bodnar, an independent developer based in Slovakia.

Common questions

How long does a roll take to develop?

24 hours after you drop it off at the lab, or 8 hours with Disposable Pro. You'll get a notification when the prints are ready, if notifications are on.

What does Disposable Pro include?

The 8-hour lab, and Reprints: printing any of your shots again on a different film, with or without the light leak. Pro is available as a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I restore Disposable Pro on a new iPhone?

Open the Darkroom, tap the price tag button at the top left, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Disposable, or tap Manage Subscription in the app's price list. To avoid being charged for the next month, cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Apple's guide: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223

Where are my photos?

They're in the app, on your iPhone. Developed prints are in the Darkroom. To keep a copy in your photo library, open a print and tap Save to Photos, or open a roll and tap Save All to Photos.

I deleted the app. Can I get my photos back?

Only photos you saved to Photos, sent to someone, or that are in a backup of your iPhone made before you deleted the app. We don't have copies: the app never sends your photos to us.

How do I send a roll to a friend?

In the Darkroom, touch and hold a roll and choose Send to a friend. Then pick AirDrop, Messages or another app. Your friend needs Disposable to open it. Roll files aren't encrypted, so only send them to people you trust.
