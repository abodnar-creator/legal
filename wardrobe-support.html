Wardrobe – Support

Need help with Wardrobe? Email contact@bdproductions.eu and we'll get back to you. If something isn't working, tell us your iPhone model and iOS version.

Wardrobe is made by Adam Bodnar, an independent developer based in Slovakia.

Common questions

The cut-out includes part of the bed or floor

Lay the piece flat on a plain surface that contrasts with it, and keep the whole piece inside the outline. In the Position step, switch to Erase and rub away anything left over. Undo steps back if you remove too much.

My photo came in sideways or upside down

Tap Rotate in the Position step. Each tap turns the piece a quarter turn.

A piece looks too big or too small in an outfit

The app sizes tops by the width of the body and trousers by the waist. It works best when the piece is laid flat with the sleeves beside it, not folded across the front.

What does Wardrobe Pro include?

Unlimited saved outfits (the free version keeps 5) and full statistics: most and least worn pieces, favourite outfits and the colours you wear. Pro is available as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I restore Wardrobe Pro on a new iPhone?

On the Today tab, tap the gear button, tap Wardrobe Pro, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Wardrobe, or tap Manage subscription in the app's settings. To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Apple's guide: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223

Where is my wardrobe stored?

In the app, on your iPhone. There is no account and no online copy.

I deleted the app. Can I get my wardrobe back?

Only from a backup of your iPhone made before you deleted the app. We don't have copies: the app never sends your clothes or outfits to us.

The weather isn't showing

Go to Settings > Apps > Wardrobe > Location and choose While Using the App. The app only uses an approximate position.

How are the statistics counted?

Every day that has an outfit planned in the calendar counts as worn once that day arrives. Plans for future days don't count yet.
