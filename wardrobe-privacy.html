Wardrobe – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how the Wardrobe app for iPhone ("Wardrobe" or "the app") handles your information. Wardrobe is made by Adam Bodnar, an independent developer based in Slovakia ("we", "us").

The short version

  • Wardrobe has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app doesn't send your photos, your wardrobe or any other personal data to us. We don't run any servers that receive data from the app.
  • Everything the app creates stays on your iPhone.
  • If you turn on the weather forecast, your approximate location is sent to Apple Weather to fetch it. Nothing else leaves your iPhone.
  • Wardrobe Pro purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details.

Who is responsible

Adam Bodnar, Slovakia. Email: contact@bdproductions.eu

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Adam Bodnar is the controller of any personal data we process. In practice, that's only messages you send us (see "If you contact us").

What the app keeps on your iPhone

The app stores the following only on your device, in storage that belongs to the app and to its widgets. We can't access it.

  • Your clothes. For each piece, the app keeps the cut-out picture it made from your photo, and the name, category, colour and style tags you gave it. The original photo is not kept.
  • Your outfits, the days you planned them for, and your packing lists.
  • Statistics about what you wear. These are calculated on your iPhone from your calendar each time you open them.
  • Your settings: accent colour, light or dark mode, and whether the morning reminder is on.
  • The latest weather forecast, so it can be shown again without asking for it each time.
  • Whether Wardrobe Pro is active.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera. Used only when you choose to photograph a piece of clothing. The photo is cut out on your iPhone and is not sent anywhere.
  • Photos. When you choose a picture from your library, you pick it in Apple's own photo picker. The app receives only the picture you picked and has no access to the rest of your library.
  • Location. Used only for the weather forecast, and only while you are using the app. See "Weather" below.
  • Notifications. Used for the optional morning reminder and for showing today's outfit on the Lock Screen. They are created on your iPhone; nothing is sent from a server.

You can change any of these at any time in Settings > Apps > Wardrobe. The app keeps working without them.

Weather

If you allow location access, the app shows a forecast for the next few days. To fetch it, the app rounds your position to roughly 10 km and sends it to Apple Weather, Apple's weather service. No name, account or other information about you is sent with it. We don't receive your location and we don't store it; only the forecast itself is kept on your iPhone.

Apple explains how Apple Weather handles this data here: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/weather/

Shopping links

When you tap a link to find similar items, the app opens a search on the shop's own website (for example Google Shopping, Zalando, ASOS or eBay). The search uses a few words that describe the piece, such as "navy shirt". Your photos are not sent. Those websites are run by other companies and have their own privacy policies. We receive nothing from them and earn no commission.

Wardrobe Pro purchases

Wardrobe Pro is sold through Apple's App Store as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment. We don't receive your name, your email address, your Apple Account details or your payment information.

The app checks your purchases on your iPhone with Apple's StoreKit, and it keeps only whether Pro is active.

  • To manage or cancel a subscription, go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, or see Apple's guide: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223
  • Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Apple gives us sales and subscription reports. They don't include your name or contact details.

What Apple may share with us

If you've turned on Share With App Developers (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and statistics about how the app is used with us, in a form that doesn't identify you. We use them only to fix problems and improve the app.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and whatever you write. We use this only to answer you and deal with your request.

  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering messages about the app (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) and, where you ask us to do something, taking the steps you requested (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Our email provider, Hostinger, stores messages on our behalf.
  • Retention: we keep emails for up to 12 months after the conversation ends, unless we need them longer to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and the app contains no third-party code (SDKs) that collects data.
  • We don't create accounts or profiles.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Everything the app stores stays on your iPhone until you delete it.
  • To delete a piece of clothing, open it in the Closet and choose Delete. This also removes it from your outfits.
  • To delete an outfit or a packing list, open it and choose Delete from the menu at the top.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your iPhone, including the widgets' data.
  • Backups of your iPhone (iCloud Backup or a computer) may contain a copy of the app's data. They follow your backup settings.
  • Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Apple Account settings, as described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access, correct or delete your personal data;
  • restrict or object to how it's used;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time.

The app keeps your data only on your iPhone, so you control it there directly. For anything we hold, such as emails you've sent us, write to contact@bdproductions.eu. We'll reply within one month.

If you're in the EU, you also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Slovakia, that's the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky), https://dataprotection.gov.sk/en/. You can also contact the authority in the country where you live or work.

Children

Wardrobe isn't directed at children under 13. The app doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

California and other US states

We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising, as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar state laws.

Security

What the app stores is kept in its own protected storage on your iPhone and is covered by the built-in protections of iOS, including the encryption iOS applies to your data when your iPhone has a passcode.
